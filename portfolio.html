GDP and Life Expectancy

The first graph looks at the relationship between GDP and life expectancy. The data is from the World Bank (countries' GDP) and the UN Population Prospects 2022 (life expectancy).

GDP Per Capita in selected countries

The following graph shows a time series of GDP per capita in 6 selected countries: China, DRC, India, Nigeria, UK and US. The analysis covers each year from 1990 to 2022.

Wine Consumption in Italy

The following graph shows the trend of wine consumption in Italy from 2006 to 2022, as a percentage of the total population over 11.

Japan Exports and Imports

The graph shows the trends of exports and imports in Japan, from 2000 to 2024. As shown by the net trade columns, the country has experienced some fluctuations in its trade balance over the years, often negative in recent years.

Italy GDP Per Capita, by Area

The graph shows the GDP per capita in Italy in 3 years, grouped by geographical area. It highlights the disparities between the Southern and the Central-Northern regions.

Review of migration graph

This section reviews a chart by Our World in Data on the total number of international immigrants, meaning people who live in a country they were not born in. It shows 2024; the data come from the United Nations Department of Economic and Social Affairs and cover 1990 to 2024. The first graph is an image from Our World in Data's website (chart).
